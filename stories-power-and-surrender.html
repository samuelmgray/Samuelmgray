Power & Surrender

Every story here contains a transfer of power, and in every one of them it moves at least once. The person who looked like they were in charge on the first page rarely is by the last.

Some of it is explicit negotiation — a contract, a safeword, an arrangement with terms. Some of it is quieter: a nurse who simply does not flinch, a transaction in a fitting room, a husband who chooses to watch. What they share is an interest in the moment control changes hands, and in what the person handing it over gets in return.
