The first page of The Night Shift

A free novella. No signup, no price — the whole thing is on this site.

Outside the large, immune-clad windows of Haukeland Hospital, snowflakes danced in the cold, gray December light. They settled like a thick, white, treacherous blanket over a city brought to a standstill. It was the same sheet of ice on the road that had sent his company car into an uncontrolled pirouette and placed him here.

Here. In this bed. In this room, with the smell of disinfectant and stale coffee, and the constant, muffled beeping of machines he couldn’t see. Trapped.

Henrik Dahl, fifty-two years old, CEO accustomed to running an international corporation with an iron fist, stared up at the sterile white ceiling and was seized by a wave of pure, unfiltered rage. Both his arms were encased in hard, white plaster from fingertip to above the elbow, resting like two useless, dead logs on the duvet.

“Nurse!” he bellowed into the shared room, ignoring the elderly man snoring in the bed beside him.

Who Is Samuel M. Gray?

Some stories are not written — they are excavated. Samuel M. Gray works in the space between confession and fiction, pulling narratives from the shadows that most writers refuse to enter. His work explores psychological dominance, forbidden arrangements, and the volatile territory where power and vulnerability collide.

Nothing is known of Gray beyond the page. He offers no interviews, no appearances, and no apologies. The work speaks. The rest is silence.

Frequently Asked

Five things readers ask before they start.

Where can I read Samuel M. Gray stories for free?

Three complete stories are free to read right here on the site, no signup required: The Night Shift (a dark erotic novella), The Beach and The Key. Newsletter subscribers also receive an exclusive free novella.

Where can I buy the books?

All published books are available worldwide on Amazon Kindle, priced $0.99–$3.99. Browse the full catalog in The Library — each book page has a free opening excerpt and a direct Amazon link.

What kind of fiction does Samuel M. Gray write?

Dark, psychological erotic short fiction: stories about desire, power dynamics, and the lines people cross. Explicit content between consenting adults, written for readers 18+. Expect slow-burn tension, games and dares, and endings that shift the balance of power.

Are the stories standalone, or should I read them in order?

Most stories are complete standalones — start anywhere. The exceptions are the series: The Black Book (read books 1–5 in order), the Posed series (start with The Bait), and The Dare Trilogy (start with The Dare).

How do I hear about new releases?

Join the newsletter. Subscribers get launch alerts before anyone else, plus an exclusive novella — The Night Shift: Maria’s Perspective — free on signup.
