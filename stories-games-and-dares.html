Games & Dares

A dare is a beautiful device, because it lets people do what they already wanted while pretending the game decided it. Nobody has to admit anything. The rules take the blame.

These are stories with structure: a first move that seems harmless, a second that costs a little more, and a point somewhere in the middle where refusing would say more than continuing. The tension is never really about what gets done — it's about who will be the first to stop pretending this is still a game.
