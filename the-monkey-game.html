The Monkey Game

Monkey see, monkey do.

  • Genre: Erotic Thriller
  • Audience: Adults 18+

About This Story

A boring Saturday night. An empty house. A random video chat with a stranger who has one rule: monkey see, monkey do.

What starts as a harmless truth-or-dare between Amalie and a mysterious, faceless man quickly escalates into something she never imagined. Each level pushes her further — past her inhibitions, past her boundaries, past the point of no return. And she doesn't want to stop.

But when the game ends and she thinks it's over, a single photo arrives on her phone. Taken from outside her window. Right now.

The Monkey Game is a scorching erotic thriller about desire, anonymity, and the terrifying thrill of being watched by someone who knows exactly who you are.
