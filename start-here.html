Questions

Common questions

Which story should I read first?

Read The Night Shift. It is a complete dark erotic novella, it is free on this site, and it needs no signup. If you would rather buy, The Bus Ride is $0.99 and is the story the whole catalogue grew out of.

Do the stories need to be read in order?

Mostly no. Fourteen of the stories and novellas are complete standalones and can be read in any order. Only three series have a required order, and they are listed above.

What is the reading order for The Black Book?

The Wildcat, then The Game, then The Exploration, then The Contract, then Queen’s Gambit. Book one is $0.99.

Are any of the stories free?

Yes — three complete stories, with no signup and no payment: The Night Shift, The Beach, The Key. Newsletter subscribers also get an exclusive novella that is not sold anywhere.

How long are they?

Most are one-sitting reads, roughly 20 to 45 minutes. The longest, Shadow Play, runs about 95 minutes.
